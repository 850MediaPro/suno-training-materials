# Fourth on the Water

Release: July 4, 2026. Follow-up to "Saltwater Sundays."

**v2, 2026-07-03.** v1 was titled "Dark-Thirty on the Fourth." Kyle listened to a generation and the hook did not land: "dark-thirty" is Franklin County event-listing jargon, and a cold listener does not decode it. Lesson captured for all future prompts: **local jargon is fuel in the verses, poison in the hook. The hook must pass the cold-listener test: someone with zero context understands it on first hearing.** v2 also strips intro vocal noodling (humming, "oh yeah" ad-libs before the first verse), names a real bait shop (Fisherman's Choice, Eastpoint, verified), and simplifies: fewer tags, no county-fireworks-schedule trivia, no history lesson in the bridge.

2. If a take still opens with vocal noodling, regenerate; do not try to fix in post. The three-layer intro guard makes clean takes common, not guaranteed.
3. Pick takes with audible breath and loose timing. Too clean = regenerate.
4. v5.5 artifacts: de-ess around 3.4 kHz, watch for style drift after 2:00, skip Suno Remaster.
5. Target 3:10 to 3:30. Chorus inside the first 45 seconds.
