# Every Weekend's the Fourth of July

Release: July 4, 2026. Follow-up to "Saltwater Sundays."

**v4, 2026-07-03.** Kyle: no dead dad (his dad is very much alive), make it about the lifestyle, keep mama fryin' mullet, and go high energy. All grief removed.
