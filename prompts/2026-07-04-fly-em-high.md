# Fly 'Em High

Release: July 4, 2026. Follow-up to "Saltwater Sundays."

**v3, 2026-07-03.** Kyle: make it playable any time of year but still mention the 4th of July, the way the famous outlaw-country "4th of July" road song pulled it off. The structural trick from that song: **the holiday is the timestamp, not the subject.** That song is a love story that happens on the 4th; this one is now a song about the old man that peaks on the 4th. The chorus states it outright: "He loved this bay three hundred sixty-five, but on the Fourth of July he came alive." Plays at a memorial, a boat day, a bonfire in January, and hits hardest on the holiday.

**Repo rule captured: to make a holiday song evergreen, aim the chorus at a person or feeling and let the holiday be the brightest memory inside it. Holiday-as-setting, not holiday-as-subject.**

v3 also generalizes the hook: "Fly 'em high, boys, fly 'em proud, for the ones who ain't here in the crowd" covers anybody's lost old man, veteran, or friend. People can post it over their own memorial and boat content all year.
