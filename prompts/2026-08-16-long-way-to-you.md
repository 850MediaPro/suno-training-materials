# Long Way To You

Barber wedding film (Grant + Elizabeth, March 7, 2026, Owl & Moon, Dixie GA). Getting-ready block: first look with dad, groom and groomsmen, bridesmaids and bride. Accepted by Kyle 2026-08-16.

**History.** v1 rejected same day: "the ai voices are too ai sounds generic and too computer generated not authentic too raspy." v1's style box asked for "husky male lead vocal with natural rasp," "slight crack at the top of phrases," and "big untrained group vocals" — the exact triggers for Suno's processed-gravel house voice. v2 kept the lyrics identical and rebuilt only the vocal direction: era tag, less-quantized, performance cues, two-friend unison. Accepted. The full doctrine extracted from this is in `shared/wedding-songs.md`.

**Why it worked in one line:** the wedding is the destination of the story, not the subject of the lyrics. Zero wedding vocabulary, machine-verified.
